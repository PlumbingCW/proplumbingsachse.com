Sachse Pro Plumbing answers calls from homeowners and businesses in Sachse, Murphy, Wylie and Garland, Texas. Our address is 5618 S State Hwy 78, Sachse, TX 75048, and the phone line is open 24 hours a day.

When you call, a dispatcher listens to the problem and schedules a licensed plumber for the earliest slot available. The plumber gives you a price before work starts. That is the whole process, and we try to keep it that simple.

What we do

We schedule emergency and planned plumbing work. That includes drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, sewer line repair, fixtures, disposals and installation. Every service has its own page that explains the signs of the problem, the steps and what affects the price.

How we keep information honest

  • We do not publish water hardness figures or code rules unless we have read them in a primary source.
  • When we are not sure, we say to test your tap or ask your water utility.
  • We ask you to check the license of any plumber you hire through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners lookup.
  • Permit questions go to the City of Sachse building department, because rules vary by project.

Where we work

Sachse is in Dallas County, and the dispatch area includes nearby towns. See our pages for Murphy, Wylie and Garland. The neighborhoods of Heritage Park, Woodbridge, Sachse Farms and Jackson Meadows are among the Sachse areas we serve.

Emergency and planned work

An emergency is any problem where water is running, a drain is overflowing, a heater has failed or something is unsafe. Those calls go to the front of the line. Planned work, such as a new water heater, fixtures or a repipe, is scheduled by appointment, and same-day visits are offered when the day allows.

If you are not sure which one you have, call and describe it. The dispatcher will tell you honestly whether it can wait until tomorrow.

What we do not do

  • We do not promise a specific arrival time, because traffic and the work already in progress change it.
  • We do not quote a firm price over the phone for a problem nobody has seen yet.
  • We do not take on large industrial plumbing systems.
  • We do not post made-up reviews, awards or ratings on this site.

Who answers the phone

The line is staffed by dispatchers whose job is to understand the problem and book the right plumber. They are not paid by how big the job is, and they will tell you when a problem can wait or when a simple step, such as closing a valve, will stop the damage. The plumber who arrives gives you the price before the work begins.

This site publishes no named staff, license numbers or review scores. When you want to confirm a plumber, ask for the license number and check it with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners.

Questions people ask us

The most common questions are whether a leak is an emergency, how to tell a clog in one drain from a clog in the main line and whether a water heater should be repaired or replaced. The pages on leak detection, sewer line cleaning and water heater replacement answer those in detail.

We also publish plain-English guides in the plumbing blog, on topics such as smelly drains, slab plumbing and water heater parts.
