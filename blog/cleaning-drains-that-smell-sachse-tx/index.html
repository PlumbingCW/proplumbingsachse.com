Cleaning Drains That Smell in Sachse, TX

A smelly drain is a message. The odor tells you whether the problem is buildup in the pipe, a dry trap or something worse in the line.

By the Sachse Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 2026
Talk to a plumber
(972) 903-8224
Call Now

A bad smell from a drain is one of the most common reasons people call a plumber for a drain that still flows. The water goes down, but the smell comes up. The cause can be simple, such as a dry trap, or more serious, such as a break in the sewer line.

This guide walks through the five usual causes, a clean-up routine that works and the signs that a plumber should look.

Five common causes

Buildup in the pipe

Grease, soap and hair coat the pipe wall and rot. The smell is musty or like rotten food.

A dry trap

The U-shaped trap holds water to block sewer gas. In a rarely used drain it dries out, and gas rises.

A dirty disposal or overflow

Food residue in a disposal, or gunk in a sink overflow slot.

A venting problem

A blocked vent makes the trap lose its water seal and gurgle.

A damaged sewer line

A crack or break lets gas escape, and the smell shows at several drains.

Match the smell to the cause

SmellOften means
Rotten eggSewer gas from a dry trap or a vent problem
Musty or sourBiofilm and buildup on the pipe wall
Rotting foodResidue in a disposal or kitchen line
Sewage, in several drainsMain line problem

A clean-up routine that works

1

Run water

Fill the trap in any drain that has not been used lately. A dry trap is the cheapest fix.

2

Clean the visible parts

Pull the stopper and clear hair and film from it. Scrub the overflow hole with a small brush.

3

Flush with hot water and soap

A kettle of very hot, not boiling, water followed by dish soap loosens grease in a kitchen drain.

4

Use baking soda and vinegar

Pour baking soda, then vinegar, let it fizz and flush with warm water. It freshens, but it does not clear a real clog.

Skip harsh chemical cleaners. They can damage pipe, burn skin and leave a hazard for the plumber who comes next.

When the smell means a bigger problem

  • The smell is in several drains at once
  • Water backs up in a tub or floor drain when you use a sink
  • You hear gurgling in the toilet or tub
  • You smell sewage in the yard or by the cleanout
  • The odor returns within a day or two of cleaning

These point to the main line. A camera inspection shows the cause, and sewer line cleaning clears a main line blockage.

What a plumber does that you cannot

A plumber can run a cable through the branch line to remove buildup you cannot reach, scour the pipe with a hydro jet and check the vents. Our drain cleaning page explains the process, and the camera reveals what a smell test cannot.

Sachse homes in Heritage Park, Woodbridge, Sachse Farms and Jackson Meadows have a mix of kitchen, bath and laundry drains that all feed a branch and then a main. For a smell that appears in one house only, the problem is probably inside the house or in the lateral line.

Keeping drains fresh

  • Run water in seldom-used drains once a week
  • Use a hair catcher in showers and tubs
  • Wipe out grease from pans before washing
  • Flush kitchen drains with hot water and soap once a month
  • Do not flush wipes, even if the label says flushable

The same steps help in homes across Garland and Wylie.

Special cases

A smell that appears only after a hot shower often comes from the tub trap or the overflow. A smell in the laundry area usually comes from the standpipe, which can dry out or hold lint. A smell from a basement or garage floor drain usually means a dry trap. A smell that appears with a gurgle after rain may point to a vent or a line problem outside.

Match the location to the cause, and clean the closest visible part first. Many smells end with a few minutes of work and a clean trap.

What a plumber can add

If you have cleaned the trap and flushed the line and the smell is still there, a plumber can check the vent on the roof, test the line with smoke or water and run a camera to find the source. These steps cost more than a cleaning, but they find causes that a household routine cannot.

Safety steps while cleaning

  • Never mix bleach with vinegar or any other cleaner, because the fumes are harmful.
  • Ventilate the room and keep pets away.
  • Wear gloves when cleaning traps.
  • If a smell comes with headaches or dizziness, leave the house and call 911.

Common questions

Why does my drain smell like rotten eggs?

A dry trap or a venting problem is the common cause. Run water in the drain first.

Is a sewer smell dangerous?

Sewer gas can be unpleasant and in large amounts harmful. A persistent smell should be checked.

Does baking soda and vinegar clear a clog?

It freshens a drain but does not remove a real clog.

Why does the basement or garage drain smell?

Floor drains dry out when unused. Pour in water and a little cooking oil to slow evaporation.
