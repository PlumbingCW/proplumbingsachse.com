Garbage Disposal Repair for Sachse, TX Homes

Most disposal failures come down to a jam, a leak or a clog past the unit. Here is how to tell which one you have and what is safe to try before you call.

By the Sachse Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 2026

A garbage disposal is one of the busiest appliances in a Sachse kitchen, and it fails in three ways. It jams and hums, it leaks under the sink or the sink backs up because the drain past the unit is clogged. Each one has a different fix, and knowing which you have saves time and money.

This guide covers how a disposal works, how to diagnose the problem, what you can safely try and when it is time to bring in a licensed plumber.

How a disposal works

A disposal does not have blades like a blender. It has a spinning plate with two small lugs that fling food against a stationary grind ring. Water flushes the waste through the drain. Because the motor is sealed in the housing, it fails in a few predictable ways: a jam stops the plate, a worn seal lets water out and a blocked drain makes water stay in the sink.

Freeing a jammed disposal safely

1

Switch it off at the wall

Then turn off the breaker if you plan to reach for anything. Never put a hand in the chamber.

2

Look from above

Use a flashlight and tongs or pliers to lift out a bone, a fruit pit or a utensil.

3

Turn the plate from below

Most units have a hex socket in the center of the underside. A wrench turned back and forth frees the plate.

4

Press reset and test

Press the red reset button on the base, restore power and run cold water, then test.

If it jams again right away, stop. A repeat jam can mean a worn motor or something stuck past the grind ring.

Finding the source of a leak

Dry the cabinet and the unit, then run water and watch. A leak at the top usually means the sink flange or its putty has failed. A leak at the side comes from the dishwasher hose connection. A leak at the bottom or through the body means the housing has a crack or the internal seal has worn, and the unit is replaced.

Tightening a loose drain slip nut can end a leak at a drain connection. Seals and housings are not repaired in the field, so a unit that leaks from the body goes to the curb.

When the clog is past the disposal

If water rises in one side of a double sink, or the dishwasher drains into the sink basin, the clog is probably in the drain line, not the unit. Running the disposal will not help. A cable machine clears it, and our drain cleaning page explains the process.

Repair or replace

A jam that clears and does not return is a repair. A unit that jams repeatedly, hums with no spin after the reset, leaks from the body or is around ten years old is usually better replaced. A new unit mounts with the same sink opening in many cases, and our garbage disposal repair and installation page covers what the visit includes.

Disposals in Sachse homes

Water in Sachse comes from the North Texas Municipal Water District and is described in its reports as moderately hard. Mineral scale can build on the grind ring and in the drain trap over time. Run cold water for about 15 seconds after each use and flush with ice and citrus peels now and then to loosen buildup.

The hardness at your tap can differ from the district average. To see where your home sits, ask your water utility or test your tap.

What not to put down a disposal

  • Grease and oil, which cool and coat the drain line
  • Fibrous vegetables such as celery and corn husks in large amounts
  • Coffee grounds in large amounts, which pack into a sludge
  • Pasta, rice and bread, which swell with water
  • Bones, fruit pits and hard shells, which can jam the plate

The rule is simple. If you would not put it down a sink without a disposal, do not feed it to the unit.

Common questions

Why is my garbage disposal humming but not spinning?

The plate is jammed. Switch it off, free it from below with a wrench in the socket and press the reset button.

Can I put my hand in to clear a jam?

No. Use tongs or pliers with the power off.

How long does a disposal last?

Many last 8 to 12 years, depending on use and what goes down it.

Does hard water shorten the life of a disposal in Sachse?

Scale can build on the grind ring and trap. Regular cold water flushes and cleaning reduce it, and a vinegar or citrus cleanse helps.

Why does my sink drain slowly even though the disposal runs?

The clog is usually in the branch line past the unit, where grease and food build up over time.
