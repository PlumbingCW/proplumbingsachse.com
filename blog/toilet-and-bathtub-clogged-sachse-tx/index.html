Toilet and Bathtub Both Clogged in Sachse, TX

One clogged fixture is a nuisance. A toilet and a bathtub that clog together point past the fixtures to the line they share.

By the Sachse Pro Plumbing team · Updated November 2026
Talk to a plumber
(972) 903-8224
Call Now

In most homes, the toilet, the tub and the bathroom sink all drain into the same branch line, and that branch joins the main sewer line. When a toilet and a tub clog together, the blockage is somewhere past both fixtures. Plunging each one will not fix it.

This guide explains how to confirm the diagnosis, what to do right away and what to avoid.

What to do in the first minutes

Stop using water

No flushing, showers, laundry or dishwasher. Every drop has nowhere to go.

Keep the toilet lid down

If water is rising, do not flush again. Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet.

Protect the floor

Towels at the base of the tub and toilet slow a spill. Move rugs and belongings.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected and which are fine.

Confirming it is the line, not the fixtures

What you seeWhat it means
Toilet gurgles when the tub drainsShared branch is blocked
Water appears in the tub when you flushMain line is blocked past the bathroom
Kitchen sink is slow tooBlockage is in the main
Only the toilet is blockedA fixture clog, try a plunger

The lowest drain in the house, often a tub, shower or floor drain, shows the problem first. Water chooses the easiest way out.

What not to do

  • Do not keep flushing to see if it clears
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into standing water
  • Do not use a drain snake in a toilet you plan to leave in service without care
  • Do not run the washing machine
  • Do not ignore sewage in the yard or by the cleanout

What causes a shared clog

Roots

Tree roots enter joints and catch paper and waste.

Grease and paper

Buildup narrows the pipe until it blocks.

Flushed wipes

Wipes do not break down, even when the package says they do.

A damaged pipe

A crack, offset or sag catches waste in one spot.

How the plumber clears it

1

Find the cleanout

A cleanout outside or in the house gives access to the main without pulling a toilet.

2

Cable the line

A machine with a cutting head breaks the blockage and clears the line.

3

Camera the pipe

A camera shows whether a defect caused the clog and whether it will come back.

4

Recommend the next step

Jetting, a repair or a maintenance schedule, based on what the camera shows.

Our sewer line cleaning page explains the process, and the toilet repair page covers problems at the fixture itself.

Backups in Sachse

The City of Sachse main line is 972.495.1212. If neighbors on your street also have backups, the problem may be in the public main, and the city is the place to ask. If only your house is affected, the blockage is likely on your side of the property line.

Ask the City of Sachse building department whether a sewer repair needs a permit before any digging starts.

After the line is clear

Watch for the signs listed on our drain cleaning page. A line that has clogged once with no clear cause often clogs again, so a camera look is worth the cost. We see the same pattern in Murphy and Garland.

What a plumber needs to know

  • Which fixtures are affected and which are working
  • When the problem started and whether it came on suddenly
  • Whether you have used any chemical cleaners
  • Whether there is a cleanout outside, and where
  • Whether water or sewage is visible anywhere

This helps the dispatcher decide what equipment to send and helps the plumber start work quickly.

Preventing the next backup

Flush only toilet paper and human waste. Put wipes, cotton swabs, dental floss and feminine products in the trash. Keep grease out of the kitchen drain, and use a hair catcher in the tub. If a tree stands over the sewer line, a camera check every few years shows root growth before it blocks the pipe.

These habits cost nothing, and they are the most effective way to avoid a call at midnight.

What a camera adds after the clog is cleared

Clearing a clog fixes the symptom. A camera pass afterward shows the reason it formed. A bellied section, a root intrusion at a joint or a build-up of scale each calls for a different response, and knowing which one you have turns a repeat call into a one-time fix.

If sewage is already in the house

Keep children and pets out of the area and wear gloves and boots if you must go in. Do not mix cleaning products, since some combinations make harmful fumes. Ventilate the room, remove porous items such as rugs, and tell your insurer. A plumber stops the source, and a cleanup company handles the rest.

Common questions

Why are my toilet and bathtub both clogged?

They share a branch line, and a blockage past both will back up both.

Should I plunge both?

A plunger will not clear a blockage in a shared line.

Is this an emergency?

It can be. If water or sewage is rising, stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

Can flushable wipes cause this?

Yes. They do not break down and can catch in the line.

Who do I call if my neighbors are backed up too?

Call the City of Sachse main line at 972.495.1212 to ask about the public sewer.
