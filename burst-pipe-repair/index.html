A burst pipe is the plumbing emergency that cannot wait. Water can soak drywall, flooring and insulation within minutes, and the damage grows every hour it runs. Our 24/7 emergency line is open day and night for exactly this, and a licensed plumber can be booked for the earliest slot available.

Burst pipe repair in Sachse, TX covers locating the break, stopping the flow, replacing the damaged section and testing the line. Until help arrives, you can limit the damage yourself in the first few minutes.

Burst pipe: the first ten minutes

Shut off the main water valve

Turn it clockwise until it stops. The valve is at the meter or where the line enters the house.

Cut power if water is near outlets

Turn off breakers for wet areas. Do not stand in water to reach the panel.

Open faucets to drain the lines

Opening a low faucet and a high faucet drains water still in the pipes and relieves pressure.

Move belongings and photograph damage

Lift items off the floor and take photos before cleanup for an insurance claim.

Why pipes burst in North Texas

Freezing is the best-known cause. Water expands as it turns to ice, and the pipe splits where the ice plug builds pressure. North Texas does not freeze every winter, but a hard freeze can catch homes with exposed pipes, unprotected attic runs or outdoor hose bibs.

Other causes include old galvanized pipe that has corroded through, high water pressure, a pipe that rubbed against framing and a failed fitting. The plumber looks for the cause as well as the break.

Where bursts happen

Attic and exterior wall pipes

These are the first to freeze when a cold front arrives.

Outdoor hose bibs and irrigation lines

A hose left connected can freeze and split the supply behind it.

Slab and under-floor lines

A leak here may start as a small crack and grow into a burst.

Water heater connections

Corroded fittings and failed flex lines let go without much warning.

How a burst pipe is repaired

1

Stop the water

The main valve is closed and the pipe is drained so the break can be reached.

2

Cut out the damage

The split section is cut back to sound pipe.

3

Replace the section

New copper, PEX or other approved pipe is joined with the right fittings.

4

Pressure test

The line is brought back to pressure and checked for leaks before the wall is closed.

If several pipes have failed, or the system is old galvanized or polybutylene, a repipe can be a better long-term answer than repeated repairs.

Water damage after a burst

Stopping the leak is the first step. Drying out the area is the second, and it should start quickly, because wet drywall and insulation can grow mold. A plumber repairs the pipe and an extraction or drying company handles the water damage. Ask your insurer which one they want you to call first.

Common questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The 24/7 line answers day and night, including weekends and holidays. Shut off the main valve first, then call and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from.

Where is my main water valve?

It is usually near the water meter at the street, or where the line enters the house, often in a garage or utility room. Find it today so you are ready.

Can a burst pipe be repaired without opening the wall?

Sometimes, through an access panel. Many bursts need a section of drywall opened, and the plumber tells you before it is cut.

Is it safe to turn the power back on after a burst?

Not if outlets or the panel got wet. Have a licensed electrician check before restoring power to wet areas.

Should I call the plumber or my insurance first?

Stop the water, then call the plumber. Notify your insurer soon after. They may ask for photos and receipts.
