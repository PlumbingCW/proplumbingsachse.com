Business plumbing has less margin than home plumbing. A backed-up restroom drives customers away, a leak in a ceiling threatens inventory, and a kitchen without hot water cannot serve. Our 24/7 emergency line is open day and night, and a licensed plumber can be scheduled for the earliest slot available.

Commercial plumbing in Sachse, TX covers drain and sewer clogs, water leaks, restroom fixtures, water heaters, backflow testing and grease-related drain work for small and mid-sized buildings. Larger industrial systems and specialized process piping are outside what we dispatch for, and we say so up front.

Business plumbing emergencies: what to do first

Shut off water to the area

Find the zone valve or the building main. Know where it is before you need it.

Protect staff and customers

Close restrooms with overflow, block wet floors and post a warning.

Move stock and equipment

Lift items off wet floors and cover them. Photograph damage for the insurer.

Call the landlord if you rent

Lease terms may say who is responsible and who should call the plumber.

Common commercial plumbing calls

Clogged restroom and floor drains

High traffic and the wrong materials in the line cause frequent backups.

Leaking supply lines and fixtures

Leaks in a ceiling or behind a wall may be hidden for days in a closed building.

Water heater failure

A busy kitchen or salon depends on a heater sized for the demand.

Grease-laden drain lines

Kitchens put grease into the line, and it builds on the pipe wall until it blocks.

Drains and sewer lines in a commercial building

Commercial drains are larger, longer and heavier used than home lines. A cable machine clears most clogs, and hydro jetting cuts grease and scale that a cable leaves behind. When a line keeps clogging, a camera shows why, and the sewer inspection page explains the process.

Backflow testing

A backflow preventer protects the public water supply from contamination if pressure drops. Many commercial properties and some irrigation systems must have it tested on a schedule. The test is done by a licensed tester, and the schedule is set by the water utility. For your property, ask the City of Sachse what it requires. We can dispatch for the testing call and for repairing a failed preventer.

Grease trap and kitchen drain work

A restaurant or food prep space with a grease trap or interceptor needs it cleaned on a regular cycle, and local rules set the interval. We can dispatch for the plumbing side, including clogged lines downstream of the trap. Trap pumping itself is often handled by a licensed hauler, and we will tell you if that is the right call for your problem.

Leak detection in a business

Commercial leak calls can be harder to resolve, because the water may travel far inside a building and the building may have multiple tenants. We locate the leak and report it, and we are clear when the repair falls to the building owner or another trade. Our leak detection page explains the methods.
