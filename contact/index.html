What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street

  • The make and age of a water heater, if it is involved

Emergency or planned work

For an emergency, call straight away. For planned work such as installation or repiping, call and ask for an appointment. We schedule same-day visits when the day allows.

Before you call: a quick checklist

1

Close the main valve

If water is running, turn the main valve clockwise to stop it.

2

Take a photo

A picture of the leak, the heater label or the damaged fixture helps the plumber plan the part.

3

Find the label

Water heaters show the make, model and age on a sticker on the side.

4

Clear the area

Move belongings out of the wet zone and make a path to the work area.
