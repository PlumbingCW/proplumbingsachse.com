Drain cleaning in Sachse, TX covers three jobs that look alike from the sink but are not alike underground. They are a clog in the fixture trap, a clog in the branch line behind the wall and a clog in the main line that carries the whole house to the street. Which one you have decides the tool, the time and the price.

While you wait, stop running water into the fixture and keep everyone off the lowest drains in the house. If several fixtures gurgle or back up together, treat it as a main line problem and say so when you call.

Drain clog emergencies: what to do first

Stop the water

Do not run the dishwasher, washer or shower. Every gallon you add has to go somewhere, and a stuck line sends it back up through the lowest opening.

Skip the chemical cleaner

Caustic drain openers sit in a standing clog and can burn the plumber who opens the line. Tell us if you already used one.

Check the lowest drain

A floor drain, basement shower or first floor tub that bubbles when upstairs water runs points to the main line, not the sink.

Move valuables up

If a floor drain is wet or a toilet gurgles, lift rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor before the line is opened.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Cooking fat cools inside a 1.5 inch trap arm and coats the pipe wall, then coffee grounds, rice and eggshells catch on it. The sink drains a little slower each week until one evening it stops.

A small hand-cranked or electric cable usually clears a kitchen branch line, followed by a hot water flush to carry the loosened film away. If the sink backs up into the second basin or the dishwasher air gap, the clog sits past the sink wye and the cable has to go in through a cleanout. Our garbage disposal repair page covers the case where the disposal itself is jammed or humming.

Bathroom sinks, showers and tubs

Hair and soap scum build a plug just below the strainer, usually within the first 12 inches of pipe. Pulling the stopper and clearing it by hand fixes some of them. When the water still pools, a 1/4 inch cable with a barbed tip catches what a plunger pushes deeper.

A shower or tub that drains slowly at the same time as a nearby toilet points past the fixture. In that case the clog is in the shared branch line, and the cable has to travel farther.

Main drain and cleanout clogs

The main line is the 3 or 4 inch pipe that leaves the house toward the street. Tree roots, a bellied section of pipe, wipes labelled flushable and grease all stop here. When the main clogs, the lowest drain overflows first, and a toilet flush can push water into the tub.

A plumber opens the cleanout, runs a larger sectional cable of 5/8 to 3/4 inch, and cuts through the blockage. If the cable keeps finding the same obstruction, a sewer camera shows what is there, and our sewer inspection page explains how that works.

Drain line leaks and repairs

A drain that clears but leaves a wet cabinet, a stain on the ceiling below a bathroom or a musty smell is leaking, not clogged. Failed slip joints, cracked cast iron and a loose tub drain gasket are common causes, and each is a repair, not a cleaning. The plumber finds the wet joint, replaces the fitting or section, and tests the line with water before closing the wall.

Common questions

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The dispatch line answers 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and drain backups are one of the most common reasons people call after dark. Tell the dispatcher which drains are involved and whether water is rising, and the plumber is booked for the earliest slot available.

How do I know whether it is the sink or the main line?

Run water in a tub or sink on the highest floor and watch the lowest drain. If water or gurgling shows up there, or two or more fixtures clog at once, the main line is the problem.

Is a plunger enough for a kitchen sink clog?

A plunger can free a fresh clog in a single-basin sink. When the water drains slowly again within a day or two, the blockage is farther down and a cable is the right tool.

Can chemical drain cleaners damage my pipes?

They can. Caustic cleaners generate heat and can soften older plastic joints, and they leave a hazard in standing water. A plumber would rather start with a dry drain than a pipe full of cleaner.

Do you need access to my yard cleanout?

For a main line, yes. Find the cap before we arrive if you can, and tell us if it is buried. If no cleanout exists, a toilet can be pulled to reach the line.
