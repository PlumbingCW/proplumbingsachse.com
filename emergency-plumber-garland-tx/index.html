Garland is a large Dallas County city, with parts in Collin and Rockwall counties, and it wraps around Sachse on the south and west. The city was incorporated in 1891 after the towns of Embree and Duck Creek merged. Our Sachse office sits close to the north Garland neighborhoods, and the 24/7 emergency line sends a licensed plumber to the earliest available slot.

Emergency plumber service in Garland, TX covers burst pipes, slab leaks, sewer backups, failing water heaters and stuck drains. Garland calls are more likely than most to involve older pipes, because so much of the city was built decades ago.

Garland grew in one big wave

Garland had a little over 10,000 residents in 1950 and about 81,500 by 1970. By 1980 it passed 138,000. A large share of the city's homes therefore date from the middle of the last century, which is also when supply pipes, drain lines and the connections under slabs were installed to older standards. Not every Garland house has a problem, but age raises the odds.

If you live in a Garland home that is 40 or more years old, ask the plumber what the supply line and drain are made of before work begins. Our repiping page covers the signs of failing supply pipe, and sewer line repair covers aging drains.

Seasonal trouble: what happens in a Garland winter

North Texas can see a hard freeze with little warning. Exposed pipes in garages, attics and outside walls can split, and the leak shows when the pipe thaws. Know where your main valve is before the cold arrives. If a pipe bursts, close the main and call our 24/7 line. The burst pipe repair page explains what to do.
