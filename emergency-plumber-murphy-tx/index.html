Murphy homes were built fast, and the plumbing is the same age

Murphy grew from about 3,100 residents in 2000 to about 21,000 in 2020, according to census figures. The President George Bush Turnpike opened in 1999 and home construction followed it. That means most Murphy houses went up within the same twenty years, and their original water heaters, shutoff valves and supply connections are now at similar points in their life.

A tank heater is commonly expected to last roughly 8 to 12 years. A heater installed when a Murphy house was new may have already been replaced once, and if it has not, it is due for a look. Our water heater repair page explains the warning signs, and the replacement page covers the next step.

What Murphy calls us about

Water heaters at end of life

Rumbling, rusty hot water, a pooling puddle or no hot water at all. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock.

Leaks under floors and in walls

A running meter with every fixture off, a warm spot on the floor or a sudden rise in the water bill points to a hidden leak.

Backed-up drains

Kitchen and laundry lines that clog repeatedly, or a toilet and tub that back up together.

Supply line and valve failures

Braided hoses, old shutoff valves and fill valves fail without warning and can flood a room.

Before we arrive: what to do in a Murphy plumbing emergency

Shut off the main valve

Turn the main water valve clockwise. In many homes it sits near the meter at the street or where the line enters the house.

Kill power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or a water heater, switch off the breaker for that area. Do not stand in standing water.

Clear the area

Move rugs, furniture and electronics out of the wet zone and open drawers or cabinets to let them drain.

Call the 24/7 line

Tell the dispatcher your Murphy street, the room affected and whether the water is stopped.

Where Murphy water comes from

The City of Murphy says it receives its water supply from the North Texas Municipal Water District. That is the same wholesale supplier that serves Sachse, and the district treats the water at plants in Wylie.

For hardness and mineral content at your own address, test your tap or ask the City of Murphy, since conditions can differ from one part of a system to another. If scale is building up in your heater or fixtures, our softener and filter page explains the options.

Leak detection and slab leaks in Murphy

In a house built on a slab, most supply lines run through or under the concrete. When one of them leaks, you often see the symptoms before you see the water: a hot spot on the floor, the sound of running water at night or a meter that keeps turning. The first step is a meter test with all fixtures off. Our leak detection page covers the process, and the slab leak repair page explains the repair options.
