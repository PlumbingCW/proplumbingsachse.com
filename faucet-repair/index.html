Faucet and sink repair in Sachse, TX covers kitchen and bath faucets, shutoff valves, supply lines, shower valves and trim, tub spouts and the drains that go with them. If water will not stop, close the shutoff valve under the sink, or the main valve for the house, and call.

When a fixture is an emergency

A shutoff valve that will not close

Old valves seize or break when turned. Do not force one that spins freely or leaks, and use the main valve instead.

A burst supply line

The braided line under a sink can fail without warning. Close the main valve and call.

Water from the wall near a shower

A leak behind the wall can spread. Shut the water off and avoid using the shower.

A bathtub faucet that will not stop

Water pouring into a tub is a nuisance. Water pouring through the ceiling below is an emergency.

Kitchen and bathroom faucet repair

Most faucet drips come from a worn cartridge, washer or O-ring inside the handle. The plumber identifies the style and replaces the part, which is often quicker and cheaper than replacing the faucet. When a faucet is corroded, the finish is worn or parts are no longer made, replacement is the better answer.

Shutoff valves and supply lines

Every fixture should have a shutoff valve. Old valves seize, and when they fail you must close the whole house. Replacing a valve and its supply line takes less than an hour for many fixtures, and it gives you a way to stop water at the sink the next time. Burst or leaking supply valves are a recurring call, and replacing them before they fail is cheap insurance.

Shower and tub problems

Showerhead leak

A dripping showerhead or a leak at the arm is often a worn washer or a failed seal.

Shower valve leak

A valve that drips or will not shut off needs a new cartridge or a valve repair.

Tub faucet repair

A tub spout that leaks at the wall or a diverter that will not hold needs a repair or a new fixture.

Slow shower or tub drain

Hair and soap cause most slow drains and a short cable clears them, see drain cleaning.

Faucets in Sachse homes

Sachse water is described by NTMWD as moderately hard, and scale builds up in aerators, cartridges and shower heads. Safety steps before a repair

  • Close the shutoff valve or the main before loosening anything.
  • Do not force a seized valve, because it can snap and flood the room.
  • Switch off power if water is near outlets.
  • Keep a bucket and towels under the fixture while you work.
Verify any plumber you hire with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners license lookup.

Common questions

Do you handle faucet and valve emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The 24/7 line answers at any hour. Close the shutoff valve or the main valve, then call and describe what is leaking.

Why does my faucet drip?

A worn cartridge, washer or O-ring is the usual cause. The plumber identifies the style and replaces the worn part.

Can you replace a faucet I bought?

Yes, in most cases. Bring the model number on the call so the plumber can check the fit and the parts.

Why is my shower pressure low?

A scaled showerhead or aerator is a common cause. Soaking in vinegar often helps, and a low pressure problem across the house needs a plumber.

What if the shutoff valve under my sink does not work?

Do not force it. Close the main valve and call. The plumber can replace the valve.
