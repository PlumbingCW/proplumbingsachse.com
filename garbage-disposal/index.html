Disposal emergencies: what to do first

Switch it off

A disposal that hums but does not turn has jammed. Turn the wall switch off so the motor does not burn out.

Keep your hand out

Use tongs or pliers, not fingers, if you must remove an object.

Stop running water

If the sink is full, wait. Water will not drain until the clog is cleared.

Dry the cabinet

A leak under the sink can swell cabinet wood. Empty the cabinet and dry it.

Common disposal problems

Humming but not turning

The blades are jammed. An Allen wrench in the bottom of the unit can free them, and a reset button on the base may restore power.

Leaking from the unit

A leak at the top flange, the dishwasher inlet or the body is a repair or a replacement, depending on where it starts.

Slow draining sink

The clog is past the disposal in the drain line, and a cable clears it.

Bad smell

Food residue and grease coat the chamber and baffle. Cleaning and a drain check are often enough.

Common questions

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The 24/7 line answers at any hour. Switch the disposal off and describe what you see, hear or smell.

Why is my disposal humming?

The blades are jammed. Switch it off, then free them from below with an Allen wrench or a wooden spoon handle from above, and press the reset button.

Can I put my hand in to clear it?

No. Use tongs or pliers, with the power off.

How long does a disposal last?

Many last 8 to 12 years, depending on use and what goes down it.

Can you install a disposal where I have none?

Yes, if the sink and the drain suit it. An electrician may be needed for the outlet and switch.
