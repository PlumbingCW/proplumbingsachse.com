Emergency plumber in Sachse, TX: what we cover

Sachse Pro Plumbing is an emergency plumber in Sachse, TX that answers the phone 24 hours a day. When you call, a dispatcher asks what is happening, where you are and whether water is running. A licensed plumber is then scheduled for the earliest available slot, and you hear the price before work starts.

We handle the problems that cannot wait: burst pipes, active leaks, sewer backups, clogged drains, failed water heaters, overflowing toilets and disposals that flood a sink. We also schedule planned jobs such as water heater installation, repiping and fixture work.

What to do in the first five minutes

Find and close the main valve

Turn it clockwise. It is usually near the meter or where the pipe enters the house.

Stop using drains

If water is backing up, no flushing, laundry or dishwasher until a plumber arrives.

Switch off power near water

Use the breaker if water is near outlets, appliances or a water heater.

Call the 24/7 line

Say your street, the problem and whether the water is stopped.

Plumbing in Sachse, Dallas County

Sachse is a Dallas County city with ZIP code 75048, and State Highway 78 runs through it as the main commercial road. The President George Bush Turnpike is nearby, Sachse High School is part of Garland ISD and Firewheel Town Center sits just south in Garland. Neighborhoods such as Heritage Park, Woodbridge, Sachse Farms and Jackson Meadows are among the places we serve.

Water here is supplied by the North Texas Municipal Water District, which draws from Lavon Lake and describes the water as moderately hard. Scale from that water builds up in heaters and fixtures over time, so water heater repair and replacement are among the most frequent calls. Our softener and filtration page explains the options.

Sachse also sits on expansive clay soils, according to the Dallas County Master Gardeners. Soil that swells and shrinks with moisture can stress slab plumbing, so leak detection and slab leak repair are common requests.
