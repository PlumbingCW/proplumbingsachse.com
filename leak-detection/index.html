Most leaks start small and stay hidden. A meter that moves when no water is running, a bill that jumped, a wet spot on a ceiling or a floor that feels warm all point to a pipe leaking where you cannot see it. When water is actively running into a wall or ceiling, the situation is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for it.

Leak detection in Sachse, TX is the work of finding the leak without opening more of the house than needed. The plumber uses meters and electronic tools to locate the leak, then explains the repair. The first move for you is simple: shut off the main water valve and call.

Active leak: what to do first

Shut off the main valve

The main shutoff is usually at the water meter or where the line enters the house. Turning it clockwise stops water from reaching the fixtures.

Cut power near water

If water is dripping from a ceiling fixture or near outlets, turn off the breaker for that area before touching anything.

Catch and photograph

Place a bucket under drips and take photos of the wet area, which help with insurance later.

Do not open walls yourself

Cutting into drywall can spread water and hide the source. Let the plumber locate the leak first.

Meter still running or a high water bill

This is how many leaks are found. Close every fixture, then look at the water meter. A leak indicator or a low-flow dial that keeps moving means water is going somewhere. A bill that rises without a change in the household is a sign too.

If you read the meter before you call, the plumber starts with a head start. The City of Sachse utility billing office, reached through the city's main line at 972.495.1212, can tell you how your usage has changed from month to month.

Ceiling and wall leaks

A brown stain, a bubble in paint or a damp smell is the usual first sign. The source is rarely directly above it, because water travels along framing and pipes. The plumber checks the supply and drain lines above, the water heater, the air conditioner drain line and the roof if the leak shows after rain, so you know whether the fix is a plumber or a roofer.

Under-house and crawlspace leaks

Homes with a pier and beam foundation or a crawlspace let the plumber see pipes from below. A leak there can wet the soil, raise humidity under the home and attract pests. The check covers supply lines, drains and the water heater supply, and it ends with a recommendation to dry the area and repair the pipe.

Slab leaks and underground lines

Many Dallas area homes sit on concrete slabs, and the water lines can run beneath them. The soil in the area is clay that swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and the Dallas County Master Gardeners note that plumbing leaks can add water under a house, which stresses the soil even more. That is a loop worth breaking early.

A warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water at night or a sudden drop in pressure point to a slab leak. The page on slab leak repair explains how the plumber confirms and repairs it.

Yard, irrigation and water service lines

Three signs point outdoors. One is a wet patch that stays after the rain stops. Another is an unexplained green strip of lawn. The third is a meter that runs while the sprinklers are off. Any of them can mean a leak in the line from the meter to the house, in an irrigation zone or at the meter box.

The plumber tests each section and isolates the leak. If a service line is failing, the fix may be a repair or a replacement of that line, and we can talk through both. Pool and spa leaks are a different specialty, so say so on the call.

Low water pressure

Pressure that drops in every fixture may be a leak, a failing pressure reducing valve or a partly closed main valve. A pressure gauge on a hose bib, a quick look at the regulator and a meter check separate those causes. It is a quick test that often settles the question.

Common questions

Do you handle leak detection emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at any hour. If water is running into a wall or ceiling, shut off the main valve and call right away, and the plumber is booked for the earliest slot.

How do I check my meter for a leak?

Turn off every fixture and appliance that uses water. Look at the meter. If the leak indicator spins or the dial moves, water is flowing somewhere.

Will you cut into my walls?

Detection tries to pinpoint the leak so the opening is small. Some repairs still need a section of drywall opened, and the plumber tells you before it is cut.

Is my high water bill from a leak?

It could be, but irrigation, a running toilet or changes in the household can also raise a bill. A meter check separates those causes.

How accurate is electronic leak detection?

It narrows the search to a small area, which is usually enough for a targeted repair. Some leaks need more than one method to confirm.
