Heritage Park is a residential neighborhood in Sachse that sits next to the city's 36-acre Heritage Park, at 4408 Hudson Drive. Local guides place the neighborhood streets around Rock House Road and Heritage Park Drive, on the Dallas County side of the city in ZIP code 75048.

This page is for homeowners there who have a plumbing problem now or want to understand what to watch for in a newer, larger house. To ask about service, call (972) 903-8224.

Where Heritage Park sits

The City of Sachse subdivision map from April 2023 shows Heritage Park in Phases 1, 2 and 3. The neighborhood is on the Dallas County side, so most addresses are in Garland ISD. School zones follow the county line and not the city limits, so confirm an address before you rely on that.

State Highway 78 and the President George Bush Turnpike are the two main routes out. The city park next door has a walking trail of about seven tenths of a mile, a covered playground and a pavilion that can be reserved.

Newer homes, larger floor plans

Local guides describe Heritage Park as built in multiple phases starting around 2012, with two story homes that often run 3,000 square feet or more. Larger houses usually have more bathrooms, more fixtures and longer pipe runs, which gives a leak more places to start and more places to hide.

Newer does not mean trouble free. Supply hoses, shutoff valves and fill valves can fail at any age, and a heater that works hard in a big house still wears out. The point is to find small problems before they become wet drywall.

If you see a stain under an upstairs bathroom

  1. Close the main water valve and keep fixtures off until you know the source.
  2. Switch off power to any room where water is dripping through a ceiling or light fixture.
  3. Place a bucket and move furniture and electronics out of the wet area.
  4. Take photos of the stain and the date for your records.
  5. Call (972) 903-8224 and describe where the stain is and how large it looks.

In a two story house, water from an upstairs leak travels along framing before it shows. The stain is often not directly under the source. Our leak detection page explains how a plumber narrows it down without cutting more than needed.

Call and describe where you see it and how fast it is changing.

Hot water demand in a big house

A single tank heater has to keep up with several showers, a dishwasher and laundry. If you run out of hot water when guests visit, the cause may be a failing heater or simply a unit that is too small for the house. Our repair page covers the first checks.

When replacement makes sense, some homeowners compare a larger tank, two tanks or a tankless water heater. The vent route and gas supply usually shape that choice more than the unit itself.

Fixtures and everyday repairs

Running toilets, dripping faucets and slow tubs are small calls that waste water and money. See toilet repair if a toilet runs or rocks and faucet repair for drips under a sink.

Common questions

Where is the Heritage Park neighborhood?

It sits next to Heritage Park on Hudson Drive in Sachse, around Rock House Road and Heritage Park Drive, in ZIP code 75048. Call with your street if you are unsure whether your address is covered.

Are the homes in Heritage Park new?

Local guides describe them as built in phases from around 2012. Your county appraisal record shows the year built for your address.

Why would an upstairs leak damage a downstairs ceiling?

Water follows framing and drywall by gravity, so a leak above a room often shows up as a ceiling stain below. Close the main valve first and then call.

Do you work on tankless water heaters?

Our tankless page explains the vent and gas questions that decide an install. Call to ask whether it suits your house.

How do I find my main water shutoff?

It is often near the water meter or where the line enters the house, and many homes also have a valve in the garage or utility room. Find it before you need it and turn it clockwise to close.
