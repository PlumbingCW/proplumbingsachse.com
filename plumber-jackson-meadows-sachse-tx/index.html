Jackson Meadows and Jackson Hills are neighboring residential subdivisions in Sachse, ZIP code 75048. Listings place Jackson Meadows in Dallas County. The City of Sachse subdivision map from April 2023 lists Jackson Meadows in Phases 1 through 3 and Jackson Hills in Phases 1, 2, 3A and 3B.

This page covers drains, toilets and private sewer lines for homes there. To describe a problem and ask about service, call (972) 903-8224.

Two subdivisions, one set of streets

Listing records place Jackson Meadows homes on streets such as Jackson Meadows Drive, Smithfield Court, Coldbrook Lane, Harvest Lane, Riverbirch Drive and Live Oak Drive. Local guides describe Jackson Hills as having a somewhat newer feel and more modern floor plans than older Sachse subdivisions.

A sample Phase 1 listing shows a 2003 house in Garland ISD on a lot of about 6,000 square feet. That makes many homes here more than twenty years old on compact lots, where the private sewer line from the house to the street is fairly short.

Slow drains in a house this age

Twenty years of use is plenty of time for grease, hair, wipes and soap to build up in a drain. A single slow sink usually points to a clog in that branch. If several fixtures slow down together or a tub gurgles when the washer drains, the blockage may be farther along in the main line.

Find your cleanout before you need it

Most houses have a sewer cleanout, a capped pipe near the house or the property line that gives a plumber direct access to the line. Locate it now. Keep the area clear and do not pour anything into it. If water ever rises in the cleanout, stop using water and call.

A camera run shows the private line from the cleanout toward the street connection. It cannot see into the city main. If a problem appears to be in the main, the City of Sachse is the right contact, and a plumber can tell you which side you are on.

Buying or selling in these subdivisions

Because the homes are past the twenty year mark, a camera inspection of the sewer line is a sensible part of buying or selling. It records the condition of the line and gives both sides the same facts. Nothing here suggests any problem in these subdivisions. The point is to look at the pipe instead of guessing.

Common questions

Are Jackson Meadows and Jackson Hills separate neighborhoods?

They are separate subdivisions with numbered phases on the city map, and they sit beside each other. Residents often treat them as one area.

When were Jackson Meadows homes built?

A sample listing in Phase 1 shows a 2003 home. Your county appraisal record shows the year for your address.

Why do my drains slow down together?

Several slow fixtures often mean the blockage is downstream in a shared line. A cable or jetting can clear it, and a camera can show why it keeps returning.

Who is responsible for the sewer line?

The private line from your house to the connection is generally the homeowner's. The city main is the city's. A camera inspection shows where a problem sits.

Do you serve Jackson Hills too?

Yes. Call with your street and the dispatcher will confirm coverage for your address.
