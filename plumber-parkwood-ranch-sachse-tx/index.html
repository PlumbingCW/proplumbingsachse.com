Parkwood Ranch is a single family neighborhood in Sachse, ZIP code 75048. The City of Sachse subdivision map from April 2023 lists it in two parts, Parkwood Ranch Phase 1 and Phase 2. Listing records place homes on streets such as Parkwood Drive, Texas Drive, Harlan Drive and Mohan Court.

If a fixture in your house is leaking, running or clogged, call (972) 903-8224 and describe it. This page explains the fixture and treatment calls that fit homes like these.

What we know about the neighborhood

  • It has its own homeowners association. A local guide says the Parkwood Ranch Homeowners Association was organized in 2010.
  • Listing data shows homes built from about 2006 into the 2010s.
  • Typical listings are four or five bedrooms and three or four baths, roughly 3,000 to 4,200 square feet.
  • Association rules can cover exterior changes, so check them before a project that alters the outside of the house.

Those figures come from listing aggregators and local guides, so treat them as a rough guide and check county records for any single address.

More bathrooms mean more small repairs

A four bedroom house with three or four baths can have a dozen or more water connections before you count the kitchen and laundry. Every toilet fill valve, supply hose and faucet cartridge is a part that can wear. Most of these repairs are quick when caught early and expensive when a slow drip rots a cabinet floor.

Toilets that run or rock

A flapper, a fill valve or a worn wax seal is usually the cause. See toilet repair.

Faucets that drip or spray

Cartridges and washers wear out. See faucet repair for the signs.

Disposals that hum or jam

A jam, a clog in the trap or a worn unit. See garbage disposal repair.

Check the cabinets once a month

The cheapest habit for a large house is a monthly look under every sink. Check the supply lines for beads of water, the drain trap for drips and the cabinet floor for swelling or a musty smell. Check behind toilets and around the base of the water heater too.

A small stain caught in the first week is a repair. The same stain in month six may be damaged cabinetry. If you find a spot you cannot explain, our leak detection page explains the meter test that tells you whether water is escaping somewhere unseen.

Hard water and appliances

North Texas water carries dissolved minerals, and scale can collect inside heaters, disposals and faucet screens. Ask the City of Sachse or test your own tap before you decide on treatment. A softener is a household decision and not an emergency.
