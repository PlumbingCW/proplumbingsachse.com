Woodbridge is a master-planned neighborhood in Sachse that crosses the Dallas and Collin county line and continues into the neighboring city of Wylie. This page covers the Sachse side. The Woodbridge Golf Club on Country Club Drive sits in the middle of it, and many streets curve around the course and its greenbelts.

If you live here and have a plumbing problem, call (972) 903-8224 and describe what you see. A short call is the quickest way to confirm that your address is covered and to ask about timing.

How Woodbridge is laid out

The City of Sachse subdivision map from April 2023 lists Woodbridge as a long run of numbered phases, from Phase 1 through Phase 20B, along with a separate Chateaus of Woodbridge Parkway section. That many phases tells you the community was built out over a span of years, not all at once.

Residents in the Dallas County part attend Garland ISD, and residents in the Collin County part attend Wylie ISD. The county line is also a useful clue when you ask the city or a utility about your account.

Homes from the late 1990s through the 2010s

Local sales records show Woodbridge homes with construction years from 1998 to 2017, and the golf club is reported to have opened in 1999. A house finished in 1999 is now more than 25 years old, while one finished in 2017 is under ten. That spread matters because plumbing parts wear on their own schedules.

A storage tank water heater is generally expected to last roughly 8 to 12 years, so many heaters in the older phases have probably been replaced already. Age alone does not mean something is wrong. A plumber has to look at the actual house. Start with our water heater repair page if you have rumbling, rust or a puddle.

Lots that back to the course or a greenbelt

Sales records describe lots of very different sizes in Woodbridge. A listing in Phase 3A shows a 2003 house on a lot of about 6,100 square feet, while other homes sit on lots close to an acre or back to the golf course or a greenbelt.

Bigger lots usually mean longer runs of pipe to outdoor hose bibs and irrigation, and a longer private sewer line from the house to the street connection. Know where your main shutoff valve and your sewer cleanout are. If your water bill jumps, our leak detection page explains how a meter test separates an outdoor leak from one inside the house.

Woodbridge and the Wylie line

Because Woodbridge reaches into Wylie, some neighbors have a Wylie address and a different city utility account. If that is you, our Wylie page covers that side. Either way, call with your street and the dispatcher will confirm coverage.

Sachse has been eligible for water from the North Texas Municipal Water District since the city incorporated in 1956. Your utility bill shows who serves your address and how to reach them for service questions.

Common questions

Is Woodbridge in Sachse or Wylie?

Both. The Woodbridge community spans the two cities and two counties. This page is written for the Sachse side. If you are unsure which city your street is in, your utility bill or county appraisal record will show it.

How old are the homes in Woodbridge?

Local sales records show construction years from 1998 to 2017. Your county appraisal district can confirm the year for a specific address.

Do you handle plumbing on the golf course side of Woodbridge?

Call and give your street. Plumbing inside the house is the same wherever the lot sits, and the dispatcher can confirm coverage for your address.

What should I do first when water is running in my house?

Close the main shutoff valve, turn off power near any standing water and move belongings out of the wet area. Then call and tell the dispatcher what you see.

Can I ask about price before booking?

Yes. Ask when you call. The price depends on the problem, access and parts, and you should hear it before work begins.
