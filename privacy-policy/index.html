Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call or visit this site, and what we do with it.

Talk to a plumber
(972) 903-8224
Call Now

This policy explains how Sachse Pro Plumbing handles information from phone calls and visits to this website. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By calling, you agree to that recording.

This site has no forms, no accounts and no checkout. We do not ask you to type personal information into the website.

What we collect

  • Information you give by phone, such as your name, address, phone number and the plumbing problem
  • Call records, including the number you called from, the time and the length of the call
  • Standard web server logs, such as the pages visited and the device type

How we use it

  • To schedule and carry out plumbing service
  • To train staff and review the quality of calls
  • To keep the website working and secure

Sharing

We share call details with the licensed plumbing contractor assigned to your service so the work can be done. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising cookies. If analytics are added in the future, this page will be updated.

Your choices

You can ask us not to continue a call recording by telling the dispatcher at the start of the call. You can also ask about the information we hold by calling (972) 903-8224 or emailing contact@proplumbingsachse.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is the one on this page.
