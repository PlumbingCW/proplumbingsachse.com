A main sewer line clog does not announce itself with one slow sink. It shows up as several drains slowing down together, a toilet that gurgles when the washer runs, and eventually water in the lowest tub or floor drain. By then the line is often full, and our 24/7 emergency line is open for that call.

Sewer line cleaning in Sachse, TX means clearing the pipe between your house and the sewer connection. A plumber opens the cleanout and sends a sectional cable or a jetting hose down the line to remove roots, grease or paper, then runs a camera if the clog looks like it has a cause.

While you wait, stop using water and keep everyone out of the lowest part of the house.

When the sewer line is the emergency

Water in the lowest drain

A shower stall, basement drain or first floor tub that fills when you run upstairs water means the main is blocked.

A toilet bubbling or filling

Water or air coming back through a toilet when another fixture runs is a main line warning.

Wet floor around a drain

Wastewater on the floor is a health hazard. Keep the area closed off until it is cleared and cleaned.

What clogs a sewer line

Roots are the most common cause in houses with mature trees near the line. Grease from kitchens builds on the pipe wall and traps paper. Wipes labelled flushable do not break up the way toilet paper does, and they gather at joints.

Less often, the line itself has failed. A bellied section holds water and solids that eventually form a clog. A cracked pipe lets soil into the line.

Cleanouts and Sachse homes

The main cleanout is the fast route to the line, and it is usually a capped pipe in the yard or at the house wall. If yours is buried or hidden inside a planter or sprinkler box, tell the dispatcher so we can plan for it. If there is no cleanout, a toilet can be pulled to reach the line, and adding a cleanout is a worthwhile fix.

Septic and storm drain questions

If your property has a septic system instead of a city sewer connection, say so on the call because the service is different. Storm drain inlets that clog with leaves and mud during a heavy storm are a separate system. Call us for private yard drains and the City of Sachse for street inlets.

After the line is clear

A line that clears and stays clear needs nothing more. A line that clogs again within a season needs a diagnosis. We suggest a camera inspection to find a crack, a bellied section or a root source, and then a plan to keep it clear.

Common questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The dispatch line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. Say which fixtures are backing up and whether water is rising, and the plumber is booked for the next available slot.

How do I know whether it is the main line or one sink?

A single slow sink is a branch clog. Several fixtures clogging together, or water in the lowest drain when you run upstairs water, points to the main line.

How often should a sewer line be cleaned?

Only when it clogs. A line with a root or grease pattern may benefit from a yearly check, and a camera inspection can tell you how often that should be.

Can I pour something down the drain to clear the main line?

No. Chemical cleaners do not clear a main line and they leave a hazard in standing water. Call a plumber instead.

Is a sewer cleaning the same as a repair?

No. Cleaning clears the pipe. If the camera finds damage, you move on to a repair or lining, and the plumber will explain the difference before work starts.
