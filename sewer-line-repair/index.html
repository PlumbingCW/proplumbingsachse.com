A sewer line fails slowly and then all at once. First comes a slow drain or a gurgle in a toilet, then a backup, then a wet spot in the yard. When sewage reaches the house the problem is no longer a nuisance, and our 24/7 emergency line is open day and night for it.

Sewer line repair in Sachse, TX means repairing the pipe that runs from the house to the city sewer connection. Who owns which section of the line can vary, so ask the City of Sachse where your responsibility ends before you plan a repair.

Until a plumber arrives, stop using water. Do not flush, run the washer or take a shower. Each use adds to a line that has nowhere to go.

Sewer backup: what to do first

Stop every fixture

Toilets, sinks, the washer and the dishwasher all feed the same line. Turn them off and ask the household not to use them.

Keep the area clear

Sewage on a floor is a health hazard. Keep children and pets out, and use gloves and boots if you must go in.

Find the cleanout

A cleanout cap is a short white or black pipe near the house or in the yard. Knowing where it is saves time on the visit.

Photograph the damage

Take photos of any wet floors or belongings before cleanup. Your insurer may ask for them.

Signs of a damaged sewer line

Several drains clog at once

A tub, a toilet and a sink that all drain slowly are sharing a line that is failing downstream.

Gurgling in toilets or drains

Air trapped behind a partial blockage pushes back through the nearest trap.

A sunken or wet patch in the yard

A low area or a bright green strip of grass above the line can mean a leak underneath.

Sewage smell outside

A steady odor near the cleanout or the front yard points to an opening in the pipe.

How we find the break

A sewer camera on a flexible cable goes into the cleanout. It shows the pipe wall on a monitor and carries a locator transmitter, so the plumber can trace the position above ground and measure the depth. That locates a break, an offset joint, a root mass or a bellied section without digging a test hole. The full process is on our sewer inspection page.

Pipe lining and trenchless repair

Pipe lining inserts a resin-coated liner into the old pipe and cures it into a new, smooth inner wall. It avoids a full trench, which matters when the line runs under a driveway, a patio or a mature tree.

Lining works when the host pipe is intact enough to hold the liner. A collapsed or heavily offset pipe needs a dig. The camera inspection tells which one you have, and a good plumber will say plainly when lining is not the right fit.

Roots, soil movement and old pipe in Sachse

Clay soil in the Dallas area swells when wet and shrinks in dry spells. That movement can pull a joint apart or leave a low spot in the pipe. Homeowners with mature trees near the line also see root entry at joints.

Where cast iron or clay pipe is present, age adds to the risk. Where PVC is used, a joint that was never fully seated can fail. A camera look tells you which of these you have, so the repair is aimed at the cause.

Common questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Sachse?

Yes. The 24/7 dispatch line takes sewer backup calls at any hour, including weekends and holidays. Stop water use, tell the dispatcher what is backing up and where, and a plumber is booked for the earliest available slot.

Who owns the sewer line, me or the city?

In general the private line from the house is the owner's concern and the main under the street is the city's, but the exact dividing point can vary. Ask the City of Sachse where your responsibility ends.

How do I know my sewer line is broken and not just clogged?

A clog clears with a cable and stays clear. A break keeps returning, may leave a wet or sunken yard area, and shows up on a camera.

Is pipe lining as good as replacing the pipe?

It can be, when the old pipe is still round and the camera shows only cracks or root entry. It is not a fix for a collapsed pipe.

Will my yard be torn up?

A trenchless repair keeps the yard mostly intact. An open dig does not, and the plumber should tell you what restoration to expect before work starts.
